Nurturing Bright Minds for a Happy Tomorrow

At Happy English Medium School, we create a joyful and secure environment where young children discover, learn, and grow with confidence. Every child is encouraged to explore their potential at their own pace.

Our Play-Way learning approach combines fun, creativity, and essential skills to help your child grow with strong values, natural curiosity, and a love for learning—guided by modern teaching methods and caring traditions.

Why Parents Choose Us

  • Loving, Experienced & Trained Teachers
  • Smart Classrooms with Interactive Learning
  • Strong Parent - Teacher Partnership
  • Emphasis on Good Habits, Values & Social Skills

Life at School

A glimpse into the daily joy and learning at our campus.

Art & Craft

Our hands-on art activities encourage imagination, fine motor development, and self-expression. Through guided creative play, children explore colors, textures, and materials while building confidence and collaboration skills.

Outdoor play

Our outdoor play experiences promote physical strength, coordination, and confidence. Through safe, supervised play, children develop motor skills and social interaction.

Friendships & fun

We nurture strong friendships through play-based experiences that encourage sharing, empathy, and teamwork. In a supportive environment, children build lasting confidence.

Creative learning

Our creative learning approach blends play and discovery to support early thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration through engaging classroom experiences.

Team Spirit

We encourage kids to participate in team sports and activities, learning the value of collaboration, healthy competition, and mutual respect.
