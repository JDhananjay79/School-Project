Since 2017

Nurturing Bright Minds for a Happy Tomorrow

At Happy English Medium School, we create a joyful and secure environment where young children discover, learn, and grow with confidence. Every child is encouraged to explore their potential at their own pace.

Our Play-Way learning approach blends fun, creativity, and foundational skills, helping children develop strong values, curiosity, and a lifelong love for learning—supported by modern teaching tools and traditional care.

Why Parents Choose Us

  • Loving, Experienced & Trained Teachers
  • Smart Classrooms with Interactive Learning
  • Strong Parent–Teacher Partnership
  • Emphasis on Good Habits, Values & Social Skills

Life at School

A glimpse into the daily joy and learning at our campus.

Art & Craft

Our hands-on art activities encourage imagination, fine motor development, and self-expression. Through guided creative play, children explore colors, textures, and materials while building confidence and collaboration skills in a joyful learning environment.

Outdoor play

Our outdoor play experiences promote physical strength, coordination, and confidence. Through safe, supervised play, children develop motor skills, resilience, and social interaction while enjoying fresh air and active exploration.

Friendships & fun

We nurture strong friendships through play-based experiences that encourage sharing, empathy, and teamwork. In a supportive environment, children build social confidence and emotional skills that form the foundation for lifelong relationships.

Creative learning

Our creative learning approach blends play and discovery to support early thinking, problem-solving, and collaboration. Children learn by doing—exploring ideas, materials, and concepts in a nurturing, engaging classroom environment.
